About.

Syed Muhammad Usama is a computational scientist at the Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Ph.D. in mathematics, KFUPM.

Email ORCID

I work where fluids mix and molecules react: direct numerical simulation of turbulence, kinematic simulation of inertial particles, quantum wavepackets for triatomic scattering, and physics-constrained learning that makes those simulations cheaper to live with.

The current appointment is at the State Key Laboratory of Molecular Reaction Dynamics and the Center for Theoretical and Computational Chemistry at DICP in Dalian.

Before China I spent five years at KFUPM in Dhahran as Lecturer-B and R&D specialist: industrial CFD for mixers, wellbores, heat exchangers and reservoirs, plus the Ph.D. on 3D sparse-grid turbulence and inertial particle-pair diffusion.

Earlier I lectured at COMSATS University Islamabad (2010–2014), after an M.S. and B.S. in mathematics there.
