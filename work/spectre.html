SPECTRE — mixing-layer emulator

A scale-separated, physics-constrained network trained on DNS mixing layers from Re 2,500 to 30,000.

Status
Under review, Results in Engineering, 2026
Code
FlowDat/SMLG
Data
Zenodo 21234013

Mixing layers are simple enough to DNS and hard enough to matter: two streams, a shear, a scalar, a growing thickness. A network that only fits one Reynolds number has learned a picture, not a flow.

SPECTRE is trained on DNS at seven Reynolds numbers — 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, 20,000, 25,000, 30,000. Stored fields: streamwise and wall-normal velocity, pressure, scalar concentration, mean, temporal ROM coefficients, reconstruction. The public analysis stack plots spectra, stresses, coherent structures, and mixing metrics against the original DNS.

The manuscript Scale-separated, physics constrained, deep neural network for turbulent mixing layer simulations is under review. Copy the Zenodo dump into data/ and the SMLG scripts regenerate the figures.
