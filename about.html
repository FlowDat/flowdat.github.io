Biography

About.

Syed Muhammad Usama is a computational scientist at the Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Ph.D. in mathematics, KFUPM. Principal Scientist, 2023.

Download CV (PDF) Email

I work where fluids mix and molecules react: direct numerical simulation of turbulence, kinematic simulation of inertial particles, quantum wavepackets for triatomic scattering, and physics-constrained learning that makes those simulations cheaper to live with.

The current home is the State Key Laboratory of Molecular Reaction Dynamics and the Center for Theoretical and Computational Chemistry at DICP in Dalian — Nature Index rank 1 worldwide. I arrived in 2019 as a Young International Talent, was named Principal Scientist in 2023, and continue as a research scientist on NSFC-supported work.

Before China I spent five years at KFUPM in Dhahran as Lecturer-B and R&D specialist: industrial CFD for mixers, wellbores, heat exchangers and reservoirs, plus the Ph.D. that became the 3DS and particle-pair programme. The Almarai Best Thesis Award (2019) and a first-prize research prize (2018) came out of that period, as did an EUHIT exchange at the Max Planck Institute and an HPC/AI allocation at KAUST.

Still earlier I lectured at COMSATS University Islamabad (2010–2014), after an M.S. and B.S. in mathematics there. Teaching has never been a side job: distinctive recognition as a TA at KFUPM, a data-science trainer, a COMSATS faculty-development certificate, and a mathematics olympiad I helped run.
