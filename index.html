Turbulence · HPC

Burst-buffer DNS of 3D sparse grids

A 3DSG with 24% blockage matches the turbulence intensity of classical and fractal grids that block far more of the tunnel. The burst-buffer algorithm cut wall time fivefold and raised parallel efficiency eightfold.

Computers & Fluids, 2026Re = 4000 · GitHub/FlowDat/3DS

03 — Now

State Key Laboratory of Molecular Reaction Dynamics.

I am a computational scientist at DICP, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in Dalian. The lab sits at the intersection of fluid mechanics, combustion, and quantum chemistry — Nature Index rank 1 worldwide.

Before Dalian I was Lecturer-B at KFUPM, where the Almarai Best Thesis Award recognized my Ph.D. on 3D sparse-grid turbulence and inertial particle-pair diffusion. Earlier I taught and consulted at COMSATS Islamabad. The through-line is the same: efficient numerics for flows that mix, burn, or scatter.
