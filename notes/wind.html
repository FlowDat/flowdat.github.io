Blade flow control and POD

Blade-resolved CFD, passive and active devices, SST closures, and a POD/ROM stack of the near-blade field.

Code
FlowDat/Blade_pod
Methods
CFD · POD · ML-ROM · SST

Rotor diameter has grown faster than the materials that have to live with it. Flow-control devices — vortex generators, trailing-edge flaps, active blowing — exist to keep the boundary layer attached, to move transition, and to cut unsteady loads.

RANS, typically SST k–ω and its transition variants, covers design sweeps. Proper orthogonal decomposition compresses the near-blade field into a reduced-order model. Blade_pod is that ROM experiment.
