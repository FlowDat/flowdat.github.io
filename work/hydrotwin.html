HydroTwin Studio

The physics of CFD is hard. The workflow is harder. HydroTwin is a desktop layer that keeps the solver and hides the obstacle course.

Status
In development
Related
SPECTRE · OpenFOAM cases · SMLG

Anyone who has taught OpenFOAM has watched a capable student drown in dictionaries, meshes, and log files. The Navier–Stokes part was never the whole problem. HydroTwin Studio is my answer: a desktop environment for interactive CFD that still talks to real solvers underneath.

The scientific backbone is the same work as this site — 3DS grids, mixing layers, SPECTRE as a fast twin of DNS, reservoir and blade cases as the industrial end. The product question is narrower: can a researcher change a blockage ratio, a Stokes number, or a blade pitch and see a trustworthy field without spending the afternoon on fvSchemes?

It is not finished. It is the direction of travel.
