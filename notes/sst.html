Shear-stress transport (SST)

Menter 1994: k–ω near the wall, k–ε in free shear, blended. Still the default engineering RANS model for a reason.

Origin
Menter, 1994
Use here
Blade RANS, industrial mixing, wind

The SST model blends Wilcox k–ω (accurate in the viscous sublayer, sensitive to free-stream ω) with k–ε (better in free shear, weaker at the wall). Extra terms keep the eddy viscosity from over-predicting in adverse pressure gradients. That combination is why it is used on wings, cars, wind-turbine blades, stirred tanks, and compressible flight.

Variants in production use: improved wall treatment, SST transition, compressible SST, SST-DES and other hybrid RANS–LES forms. Blade_pod compares a KOSST closure with a POD reduced-order model of the same blade field.
