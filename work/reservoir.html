Subsurface flow · mixing

Reservoir simulation and pipe mixing

OpenFOAM cases for multiphase subsurface flow, and a pipe-mixing campaign of CoV versus Reynolds number.

Codes
Reservoir · 3DSPipe

Reservoir simulation is Darcy-scale transport, wells as internal boundary conditions, saturations that must be conservative. The Reservoir repository holds tutorial cases: single-phase setup, multiphase and well control, then domain decomposition and parallel I/O.
