Turbulent dispersion · 2017–2019

Inertial particle-pair diffusion

How massive particles, not tracers, separate in turbulence — pollen, sand, industrial aerosols.

Record
Dissertation, 2019 · AMMCS 2017 · EGU 2017
Method
Kinematic simulation (KS)

Tracers follow the fluid. Inertial particles do not. Stokes number decides whether they ride eddies or cut through them, and pair dispersion changes accordingly. Richardson’s 1926 locality picture is the textbook; a non-local theory of pair diffusion is the alternative under test.

Kinematic simulation builds synthetic turbulence cheap enough for millions of pairs and rich enough in scales to distinguish local from non-local spreading. Low-St particles behave like dyes. High-St particles filter small eddies and remember ballistic stretches.
