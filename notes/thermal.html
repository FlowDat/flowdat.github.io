Multiscale battery thermal management

Lithium-ion packs fail thermally at the cell, the module, and the chemistry at once. The diagnosis has to as well.

Methods
CFD heat transfer · computational chemistry · multiscale entropy

Battery thermal management is not only a cooling problem. Thermal runaway is a chemical cascade that CFD alone cannot see and that a 0D equivalent-circuit model will not locate in a pack. The useful picture is multiscale: CFD for coolant and cell-to-cell coupling; computational chemistry for the reactions that start the runaway; data-driven diagnostics (modified multiscale entropy on temperature traces) for the pattern that says the pack is leaving the safe manifold.
