Generative models for inorganic crystals

Diffusion models that propose stable, unique, novel structures instead of screening a finite database.

Context
MatterGen (Microsoft Research, Nature) · QMD notes

Materials discovery is usually a search over known crystals. Generative diffusion models invert that: start from noise, denoise into a chemically valid lattice, optionally conditioned on bulk modulus, magnetism, or adsorption. MatterGen, trained on hundreds of thousands of known inorganics, has produced S.U.N. (stable, unique, novel) candidates and at least one experimentally realised bulk-modulus target.

The design space of interest in these notes is carbon-capture frameworks (MOFs, zeolites), energy-storage solids, and corrosion-resistant alloys — the same molecular-dynamics toolchain (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, LAMMPS, Gaussian) that sits next to the stair-grid scattering work.
